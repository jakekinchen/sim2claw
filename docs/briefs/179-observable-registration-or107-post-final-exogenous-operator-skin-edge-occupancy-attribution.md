# OR107 — Exogenous operator skin-edge occupancy attribution

The retained physical process is visibly operated by a human hand and forearm;
the renderer contains neither. OR107 quantifies that operational gap before any
actor geometry is introduced.

One fixed, generic HSV-and-YCrCb color proxy is frozen in the contract. It is
applied outside the board region to the same three temporal samples per episode
used by OR104 and OR106. The diagnostic measures proxy area and the fraction of
physical edges associated with its dilated support. It makes no person identity
or biometric inference.

Seven development episodes must establish material occupancy before the four
already-open validation episodes are decoded under the exact same proxy and
thresholds. No candidate video is decoded; no render, fit, parameter value,
replay, action/state mutation, hardware, or paid compute is allowed.

A pass attributes materially present exogenous operator content and may freeze
one deterministic renderer-native operator-proxy diagnostic. It does not
calibrate hand geometry or trajectory, prove same-video semantics, physics,
transfer, or simulator promotion.
