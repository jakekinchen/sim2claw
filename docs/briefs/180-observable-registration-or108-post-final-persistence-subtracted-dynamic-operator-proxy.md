# OR108 — Persistence-subtracted dynamic operator proxy

OR107's fixed color proxy was numerically strong but visually confounded by the
wooden board frame. OR108 fits exactly one nuisance support: pixels labeled by
the same proxy in at least 80% of the 21 development samples. That persistent
support is dilated by five pixels, subtracted, and frozen before validation.

Dynamic proxy area and associated physical-edge occupancy are then measured on
development and on all 12 no-refit validation samples. The persistent support
must itself occupy a bounded 4–9% of the outside-board region, preventing an
empty or wholesale subtraction.

This is a diagnostic pixel-space nuisance factorization, not a simulator
change. No identity or biometric inference, candidate decode, render, replay,
hardware, or paid compute occurs. A pass may justify one renderer-native
exogenous actor proxy, but cannot establish hand geometry, trajectory,
same-video semantics, physics, transfer, or simulator promotion.
