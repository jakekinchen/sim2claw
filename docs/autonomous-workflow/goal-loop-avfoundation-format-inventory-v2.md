## Decision Status

- V1 consumed its one observation and failed in `JSONSerialization` on
  `__SwiftValue`.
- V1 wrote no raw inventory and supports no format/rate claim.
- The v1 source used heterogeneous `[String: Any]`.
- Swift `Codable` plus `JSONEncoder` avoids that bridging path.

### Assumptions

- The exact-name C922 remains attached and camera authorization remains
  granted.
- Converting `AVCaptureColorSpace.rawValue` to `Int` removes the observed
  bridge failure, but v2 must still fail closed if another runtime prerequisite
  appears.

### Recommended Defaults

- Preserve the selection rule and one-observation budget.
- Persist the attempt before launch.
- Treat absent raw output as abstention, never as empty inventory.

### Open Questions

- The actual C922 native dimensions, subtypes, and rates.
- Whether a 640×480 candidate lies within 0.05 fps of 30.
